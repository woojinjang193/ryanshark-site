GenePet: Cat Fairy Raising

A cozy raising game where you combine genes to create your own cat fairy

GenePet is a cozy raising game where you design the cat fairy you want by combining genes, then raise and care for it. Mix and match parts to shape its look and personality, and watch it change as it grows.

  • Gene combining — design looks and personality from a wide range of parts
  • 100B+ combinations — a cat fairy that's one of a kind
  • Grow & care — watch your fairy change as it grows
